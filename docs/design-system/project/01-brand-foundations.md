# 01 — Brand foundations

The one-page brief for everyone writing copy or shipping components.

## Who we are

Beyond Service is a B2B Integrated Facility Management and project execution organisation. We work for corporate and industrial clients: factories, campuses, logistics parks, retail chains, data centres and multi-site occupiers. We are not a consumer service, not a ticketing app, and not a marketplace that lists vendors for discovery. We take responsibility for the engagement end to end — scoping, mobilising vendors, running the job, reporting back.

## What we stand for

1. **Project certainty.** We finish on time, within budget, to spec. Everything in copy and interface should feel like we have a plan.
2. **Technical competence.** We work to IS, NBC, ASHRAE, ECBC and local fire NOC norms. We mention standards where they matter.
3. **PAN India reach.** We serve 14+ states with a vetted provider network. This is why clients consolidate on us.
4. **Dependable communication.** Dedicated account manager, structured reporting, no silence.

## Who we talk to

- Facility and operations heads of large companies.
- Procurement teams running multi-site maintenance contracts.
- Project directors handling plant expansion, office fit-out or infra rollout.
- Occupiers consolidating many soft-service vendors.

They are busy, risk-averse, and have seen enough bad vendors to be suspicious of marketing. They respond to specifics.

## Voice

- **Direct.** State the service, the scope, the standard, the SLA. Lead with the noun, not the adjective.
- **Confident, not boastful.** "We deliver" beats "best-in-class". Numbers beat superlatives.
- **Operational.** Verbs over moods. Capabilities over feelings.
- **Indian English.** PAN India, not PAN-India. Lakh/crore for monetary ranges, not millions. Rs or ₹, not INR.

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Hero and section headlines | Declarative, short, business-focused. One concrete claim per line. |
| Service descriptions | Clear, technical, with standards and brands named. |
| CTAs | Imperative verbs. "Get a Quote", "Request Quotation", "Speak to Our Team". |
| FAQ answers | Plain, exact, 1–3 sentences. If the honest answer is "it depends", say what it depends on. |
| Error and empty states | Flat and helpful. Never cute. |
| Testimonials | Verbatim, attributed, verified. Never improved by us. |

## Writing rules

**Do**

- Lead the headline with the business outcome, not the service name.
- Use the active voice.
- Name the standard where it strengthens the claim (IS, NBC, ASHRAE, ECBC, Fire NOC).
- Name the brand where we're a certified installer or service partner (Daikin, Hikvision, Legrand, Cummins etc.).
- Write ranges in rupees as "₹10 lakh to ₹10 crore".
- Describe the engagement model, not just the vendor list.

**Don't**

- Use "world-class", "cutting-edge", "best-in-class", "premier".
- Mention awards, certifications or clients we have not confirmed in `20-implementation-and-verification.md`.
- Invent testimonials, team bios, titles, signatures or founder quotes.
- Use "we help you" filler. Say what we actually do.
- Use emoji in any body copy or UI label.
- Imply we are an in-house workforce where the model is vetted-provider execution. The honest word is "partner".

## Name and spelling conventions

- Company name: **Beyond Service** (two words, capitalised).
- Not "BeyondService" except as the component namespace, not "Beyond Services".
- Services get title case: **Civil Project & R&M Work**, **MEP Services**, **ELV Services**.
- Use "and" in prose, "&" in short labels only (navigation, cards, breadcrumbs).
- Metrics format: `14+ States`, `100+ Corporate Clients`, `500+ Service Providers`, `9+ Verticals`. The `+` is attached, no space.
- Phone format: `+91 79847 87910`. Keep the space grouping consistent across the site.
- Address format: one line in-line, broken by commas: `6th Floor, 619, Vesu Main Road, near S D Jain School, Vesu, Surat, Gujarat 395007`.

## Design principles

1. **Architecture over decoration.** Every surface earns its place. If a card, divider or icon does nothing new, drop it.
2. **One accent.** Champagne is the only accent colour. Nothing else highlights.
3. **Photography carries the mood.** Real facility, real operations. No mock datacenter stock, no abstract vector cities.
4. **Type is the picture, often.** On a text-only section, let the type do the work rather than adding visuals to fill space.
5. **Rhythm, not symmetry.** Section padding varies with content weight. Not every section is the same height.
6. **The hero sets the tone.** The hero is dark; the rest of the site can breathe on cream.

## Palette rules in one line

Charcoal and cream dominate. Champagne accents. No navy, no safety-amber, no multi-colour gradients, no neon.

## Grid and container rules

- Max container: 1280px for standard sections, 1440px for the hero and dark CTA strip.
- Gutters: 24px desktop, 16px mobile.
- Section vertical padding: 96px desktop / 64px tablet / 80px mobile for a standard section; the hero takes 128–160px top and bottom on desktop.
- No section should be exactly the same height as the one above it.
