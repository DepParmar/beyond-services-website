## 2. Who we are

Prose section, single column, max-width ~720px for readability.

**Heading (`section-h1`):**
Who we are.

**Body (`body-lg`):**
Beyond Service was founded in 2019 to simplify how corporate and industrial organisations in India manage their facilities. We saw clients juggling dozens of small vendors across locations — negotiating, chasing, auditing, often re-hiring. We built an operating model where one Beyond Service account manager runs the end-to-end engagement, drawing from a vetted network of 500+ specialist service providers across 14+ states.

Today we deliver civil, MEP, ELV, fire safety, cooling, interior, industrial equipment, logistics and project management services to 100+ corporate clients. Our registered office is in Surat; our teams operate across every major Tier 1 and Tier 2 Indian city.

## 4. What we do differently

Four-tile grid. Short headlines, two-sentence descriptions.

**Eyebrow:** OUR APPROACH

**Heading (`section-h1`):**
Four things that change the engagement.

### 1. One account manager, nine disciplines

Our account managers coordinate across every service — so you brief one person, not nine specialists. Internal handovers between civil, MEP and ELV teams become our problem, not yours.

### 2. Vetted provider network

Every vendor in our 500+ provider network is pre-qualified on technical competence, documentation and conduct. We re-score providers on each engagement — poor performers leave the network.

### 3. Standards and compliance built in

We work to IS codes, NBC, ASHRAE and ECBC norms, and we handle fire NOC paperwork end-to-end. Compliance is scoped at kick-off, not patched at handover.

### 4. Structured communication

Weekly dashboards, defined reporting cadence, closeout documentation. No surprises, no silence. If a milestone slips, you hear it from us first with the recovery plan attached.

## 5. Our approach

A short visual sequence showing how an engagement runs end to end. Treat as a 5-step horizontal process block on desktop, a vertical stepper on mobile.

**Eyebrow:** FROM SCOPE TO HANDOVER

| # | Step | What happens |
|---|------|---|
| 01 | Brief | We take a scope call, review your site(s), understand constraints and timelines. |
| 02 | Scope and quotation | We issue a detailed scope, BOQ where relevant, and a fixed quotation with payment milestones. |
| 03 | Mobilise | We select providers from our vetted network, align them to your site rules, and schedule mobilisation. |
| 04 | Execute | Our account manager runs the engagement — daily on-site coordination, weekly client reporting. |
| 05 | Handover | We close out with final inspection, snag resolution, documentation and (where relevant) AMC proposal. |
