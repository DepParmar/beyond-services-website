## 4. Our engagement model

Three-up grid with icon-less cards. Written to answer the "how do we work with you?" question that every procurement reader has.

**Eyebrow:** HOW WE ENGAGE

**Heading (`section-h1`):**
Three ways to work with Beyond Service.

### 1. Single-project engagement

A one-off scope — a fit-out, a facility overhaul, an equipment install. We scope, quote, mobilise, deliver and hand over. Suited to a one-site, time-bound requirement.

### 2. Annual maintenance contract (AMC)

A recurring scope across one or many sites — civil R&M, MEP maintenance, cooling AMC, fire system AMC. Includes PM visits, breakdown support, monthly reporting and quarterly reviews.

### 3. Project management consultancy (PMC)

We own the delivery of a client-funded capital project — scope, vendor selection, cost control, timeline, quality — reporting into your project sponsor. Suitable for ₹1 crore to ₹10 crore+ projects.
