## 2. Overview

**Heading:** End-to-end MEP for every facility type.

**Body:**
Our MEP division provides complete Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing services for corporate campuses, industrial plants, retail chains and commercial buildings. From initial design coordination to installation, testing and commissioning — we handle the full engineering stack.

We work with certified engineers and licensed contractors who deliver to IS codes and NBC standards. Scope includes HT and LT panels, power distribution, wiring, earthing, HVAC and ducting, pumping and water treatment, STP and fire-side plumbing. Our engagements are structured for compliance with safety regulations and optimum energy efficiency over the asset's life.
