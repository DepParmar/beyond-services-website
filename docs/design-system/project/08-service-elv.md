## 2. Overview

**Heading:** Reliable, scalable ELV systems that grow with your business.

**Body:**
Beyond Service provides comprehensive ELV (Extra Low Voltage) system solutions for commercial and industrial clients. Scope includes CCTV surveillance, structured cabling, networking, access control, video door phones, PA systems, EPABX, BMS integration and fire alarm integration.

Our ELV team comprises certified professionals trained on leading brand ecosystems — Hikvision, Bosch, Honeywell, Legrand, Schneider and others. We select the platform based on your scale, integration needs and operational budget rather than committing you to a single vendor. Every system is designed to be scalable — adding cameras, doors or data runs as your facility grows.
