## 2. Overview

**Heading:** Turnkey interiors — from drawing to walkthrough.

**Body:**
We transform corporate spaces through comprehensive civil interior services. Scope covers false ceiling (grid, gypsum, POP, acoustic), partition walls (gypsum, glass, modular), glass and glazing, modular furniture installation, flooring (vitrified, wooden, vinyl, carpet, epoxy), painting, wallpaper and the full catalogue of fit-out works.

Our interior teams bring together skilled carpenters, civil contractors, glaziers, electricians and HVAC specialists to deliver turnkey office refurbishments and new fit-outs. We have delivered interior projects for corporates across Maharashtra, Gujarat, Karnataka and Delhi NCR.

## 6. FAQs

**Do you provide turnkey interior solutions?**
Yes — we offer complete turnkey interior services from design consultation and material procurement through execution to final handover. One accountable team, one quotation, one timeline.

**Can you work in an occupied office?**
VERIFY — original FAQ answer was not visible. Likely: Yes, we plan phased, zoned or after-hours execution so your team can continue working during the fit-out. We agree site rules and safety protocols with your facility manager before mobilisation. Confirm with Beyond Service.

**What is the typical timeline for an office fit-out?**
VERIFY — original FAQ answer was not visible. Likely: Small refurbishments (under 5,000 sq ft) run 4–6 weeks; typical corporate fit-outs 8–12 weeks; large and complex fit-outs can take 14–20 weeks. Timelines firm at BOQ sign-off. Confirm bands with Beyond Service.

**Do you handle MEP as part of a fit-out?**
Yes — MEP works are coordinated as part of the civil interior scope. We handle electrical, data, HVAC, fire-side and plumbing alterations through the same account manager.
