## 2. Overview

**Heading:** Energy-efficient cooling for every facility class.

**Body:**
Beyond Service provides installation, maintenance and AMC services for all cooling systems — split ACs, cassette ACs, ducted systems, VRF and VRV systems, chillers, cooling towers, precision cooling for data centres and centralised HVAC.

Our refrigeration and HVAC engineers are certified on leading brands including Daikin, Voltas, Blue Star, Carrier and Hitachi. We size and install systems to ASHRAE standards and ECBC compliance where applicable, and the delivered systems carry OEM warranties alongside our installation warranty.

## 5. Our two-step process

### Step 01 — Load calculation and system design

- Heat load calculation per zone
- System selection (DX vs VRF vs chilled water)
- Energy analysis for operating cost
- Layout planning with equipment and piping routes

### Step 02 — Installation and commissioning

- Equipment installation per approved drawings
- Refrigerant charging and leak-testing
- Controls programming and BMS integration where applicable
- Performance testing against design conditions
