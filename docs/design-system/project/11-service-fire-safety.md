## 2. Overview

**Heading:** Fire-side engineering, delivered to NBC and NOC norms.

**Body:**
Beyond Service delivers end-to-end fire and safety solutions for corporate and industrial facilities. Scope covers fire detection and alarm systems, fire suppression (sprinkler, clean agent such as FM-200, CO₂ and water mist), fire hydrant systems, portable extinguisher supply and maintenance, safety signage, and full compliance audits as per NBC and local fire NOC requirements.

Our fire-safety engineers are trained and certified; we have supported 100+ corporate clients with fire NOC compliance and recurring maintenance of their fire systems across multiple states through our PAN India network.
