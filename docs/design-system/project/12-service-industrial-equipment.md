## 2. Overview

**Heading:** Keep industrial assets running.

**Body:**
We provide installation, preventive maintenance, breakdown repair and AMC services for industrial equipment — DG sets, motors, pumps, compressors, material-handling equipment, hydraulic systems, conveyor systems and site-utility equipment.

Our industrial-service teams are trained to work in manufacturing plants, warehouses, logistics hubs and heavy industrial facilities. We offer 24/7 breakdown support to minimise downtime and production losses, with defined response-time commitments in our AMC contracts.

## 6. FAQs

**Do you provide DG set installation and servicing?**
Yes — we install, commission and service DG sets from 15 KVA to 2000 KVA for all major brands including Cummins, Kirloskar, Greaves and Mahindra. Service covers routine PM, oil and filter changes, load tests and major overhauls.
