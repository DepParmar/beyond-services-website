## 2. Overview

**Heading:** Move assets without disruption or damage.

**Body:**
Beyond Service provides logistics coordination for corporate relocations, equipment moves, material transportation, warehouse management support and last-mile delivery for facility-related projects across India.

We work with a network of trusted logistics partners — transportation companies, packers and movers, crane operators and warehousing partners — to deliver smooth, damage-free movement of assets for corporate clients. Our role is coordination and accountability: scoping the move, selecting the right partner, supervising execution and signing off on condition at delivery.
