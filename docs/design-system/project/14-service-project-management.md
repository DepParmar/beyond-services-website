**Body:**
Beyond Service's project management division acts as your trusted Project Management Consultant (PMC) — overseeing planning, vendor management, quality control, cost management, timeline adherence and stakeholder reporting for facility and infrastructure projects.

Our project managers bring experience in civil, MEP, interior and industrial projects, and run engagements through a structured reporting system that keeps your project sponsor informed at every stage. We take delivery accountability for the project — if a milestone slips, you hear it from us first with the recovery plan attached.
