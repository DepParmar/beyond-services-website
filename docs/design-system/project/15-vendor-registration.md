## 4. Vendor criteria

Shown as a four-point list with short descriptions. Pulled directly from the source copy, tightened.

### Professionalism and timeliness

You hit the dates you commit to. You communicate proactively when something changes. Your team conducts itself appropriately on client sites.

### Trained and experienced workforce

Your technicians are trained for the work; your supervisors have experience managing it. We will ask for proof.

### Quality-conscious workmanship

You take ownership of the finish, not just the turn-up. Snags are addressed without a chase cycle.

### Compliance and documentation ready

Statutory licences, GST, PAN, insurance (public liability and workmen's compensation), labour compliance and site-safety protocols — in place and shareable on request.

## 6. What happens next

Short step-list under the form.

1. **Receipt within 1 business day.** We acknowledge your application by email.
2. **Initial screening within 7 business days.** We review documentation and shortlist based on our current pipeline.
3. **Discovery call.** For shortlisted vendors, our vendor team runs a scoping call to understand your capability depth.
4. **In-person verification.** For vendors cleared through the discovery call, we invite you to visit our Surat office for a verification meeting.
5. **Onboarding.** Successful vendors are added to the network with the discipline, geography and project-size bands they're approved for.

VERIFY these response times with Beyond Service's vendor team before publishing.
